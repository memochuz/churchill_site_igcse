Throughout my education, some books have been key to shaping my mathematical thinking. **Topology** by James Munkres gave me a rigorous foundation in general topology, while his **Elements of Algebraic Topology** deepened my understanding of homology and cohomology from a formal and structured perspective. In the field of abstract algebra, **Advanced Modern Algebra** by Joseph Rotman broadened my view with an approach that spans from group theory to representations.

I hope these recommendations are useful for anyone who wants to delve into these areas, or at least, serve as an entertaining and worthwhile way to spend your time.
