1. Basic & Radical Equations — SADMEP Method

  1. Identify: Locate $x$ and list all operations affecting it.
  2. Rank: Order operations from outermost (furthest from $x$) to innermost.
  3. Undo (SADMEP): Reverse each operation in order: Subtraction/Addition, Division/Multiplication, Exponents/Radicals, Parentheses.
  4. Simplify: Perform the arithmetic steps to fully isolate $x$.

2. Rational Equations with Variables in the Denominator

  1. Clear Fractions: Multiply both sides by the denominator to clear rational terms.
  2. Expand: Apply the distributive property to remove parentheses.
  3. Collect Terms: Move all terms containing $x$ to one side and non-$x$ terms to the opposite side.
  4. Factor & Isolate: Factor out $x$ as a common factor, then divide by its coefficient.

3. Exponential and Logarithmic Equations

  1. Apply Logarithms: Take $\ln$ or $\log_b$ of both sides and apply logarithm laws:
    • $\log_{b}(A \cdot B) = \log_{b} A + \log_{b} B$
    • $\log_{b}\left(\frac{A}{B}\right) = \log_{b} A - \log_{b} B$
    • $\log_{b}(A^k) = k \cdot \log_{b} A$
  2. Expand & Group: Expand any products and group all terms containing $x$ on one side.
  3. Factor & Isolate: Factor out $x$ and isolate the variable.
