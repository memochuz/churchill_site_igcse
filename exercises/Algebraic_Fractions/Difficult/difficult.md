# Difficult 🔴

Simplify the following expressions using the formula for adding and subtracting algebraic fractions:

<p align="center"><img src="https://memochuz.github.io/churchill_site_igcse/images/Algebraic_Fractions/algebraic_fractions_2.png" width="200"></p>

> 1. $\left(\frac{x+1}{x-2} + \frac{x-1}{x-2}\right) \cdot \frac{2}{x+3}$
> 2. $\frac{2x}{x^2-1} - \left(\frac{x-1}{x+1} \cdot 3\right)$
> 3. $\left(\frac{x+3}{x^2-9} \div \frac{x-3}{x+3}\right) + \frac{2}{x-3}$
> 4. $\left(\frac{y^2-1}{y+2} + \frac{3}{y-2}\right) \cdot \frac{y}{y+2}$
> 5. $\frac{2a}{a^2-4} - \left(\frac{3}{a-2} \cdot a\right)$
> 6. $\left(\frac{m+2}{m^2-4} \div \frac{m-2}{m+2}\right) + 1$
> 7. $\frac{x^2-9}{x+3} + \left(\frac{2x}{x-3} \cdot \frac{1}{2}\right)$
> 8. $\frac{p^2-1}{p-1} - \left(\frac{2p}{p+1} \cdot 2\right)$
> 9. $\left(\frac{3x}{x^2-4} + \frac{x+2}{x^2-4}\right) \cdot \frac{x}{3}$
> 10. $\left(\frac{x^2+2x}{x^2-1} \cdot \frac{x-1}{x}\right) + 1$
