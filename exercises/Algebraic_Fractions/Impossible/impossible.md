# Impossible ⚫

Simplify the following expressions using the formula for adding and subtracting algebraic fractions:

<p align="center"><img src="https://memochuz.github.io/churchill_site_igcse/images/Algebraic_Fractions/algebraic_fractions_2.png" width="200"></p>

> 1. $\left(\frac{x^2+1}{x-1} + \frac{2x}{x+2}\right) \cdot \frac{x-2}{x+1} - \frac{3}{x}$
> 2. $\frac{x+3}{x^2-4} \div \left(\frac{x-2}{x+1} + \frac{2}{x-1}\right) - \frac{x}{x+2}$
> 3. $\left(\frac{2x}{x^2-1} - \frac{x-1}{x+1}\right) \cdot \frac{x+1}{2} + \frac{3}{x}$
> 4. $\frac{x^2-4}{x+2} + \frac{3}{x-2} \cdot \left(\frac{x+1}{x-1} - 2\right)$
> 5. $\left(\frac{x+2}{x^2-1} \div \frac{x-1}{x+1}\right) + \frac{2x}{x-2} - 1$
> 6. $\frac{x^3-1}{x^2-1} - \frac{2x}{x+1} \cdot \left(\frac{x-1}{x}\right) + \frac{1}{x-1}$
> 7. $\left(\frac{x^2+3x+2}{x^2-4} + \frac{2x-3}{x+2}\right) \div \frac{x-2}{x+1}$
> 8. $\frac{2x^2-1}{x+1} \cdot \left(\frac{x-1}{x^2-1} + \frac{3}{x}\right) - \frac{x}{x+2}$
> 9. $\left(\frac{x^2-1}{x+2} - \frac{x+1}{x-1}\right) \cdot \frac{2x}{x+1} + \frac{3}{x-2}$
> 10. $\frac{x^3+2x}{x^2-4} + \left(\frac{x^2-1}{x+1} \div \frac{x-2}{x}\right) - \frac{2}{x+3}$
