Simplify the following expressions using the formula for adding and subtracting algebraic fractions:

<p align="center"><img src="https://memochuz.github.io/churchill_site_igcse/images/Algebraic_Fractions/algebraic_fractions_2.png" width="200"></p>

> 1. $\frac{x+1}{x+2} + \frac{x-3}{x+2}$
> 2. $\frac{2x-1}{x-4} - \frac{x+5}{x-4}$
> 3. $\frac{x^2+1}{x+3} + \frac{2x}{x+3}$
> 4. $\frac{3x+2}{x-1} - \frac{x^2}{x-1}$
> 5. $\frac{x^2+2x}{x+5} + \frac{5x-3}{x+5}$
> 6. $\frac{y^2+1}{y+2} - \frac{3y-4}{y+2}$
> 7. $\frac{2a+3}{a-1} + \frac{a^2-1}{a-1}$
> 8. $\frac{m^2+2m}{m+4} - \frac{3m+5}{m+4}$
> 9. $\frac{p^2-1}{p+3} + \frac{2p+7}{p+3}$
> 10. $\frac{x^2+4}{x-2} - \frac{3x-1}{x-2}$
