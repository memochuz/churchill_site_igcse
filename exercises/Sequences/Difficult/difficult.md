#  Hard 🔴

Solve the following sequence problems:

> 1. A ball is dropped from a height of 10 meters and each time it bounces, it reaches $\frac{3}{4}$ of the previous height. Find the height after the 6th bounce.
> 2. A theater has a seating arrangement such that each row has 2 more seats than the previous row. If the first row has 12 seats, how many seats are there in the 15th row?
> 3. A person saves money in a piggy bank: $5$ dollars the first week, $8$ dollars the second week, $11$ dollars the third week, and so on. How much money will the person save in the 20th week?
> 4. A ladder has rungs that are spaced 30 cm apart. The first rung is 20 cm above the ground. Find the height of the 15th rung from the ground.
> 5. In a parking lot, the number of cars parked each day increases by 3 compared to the previous day. If 7 cars were parked on Monday, how many cars will be parked on Friday?
> 6. A bouncing ball reaches 8 meters initially, and after each bounce it reaches 80% of the previous height. Find the total distance traveled by the ball after 5 bounces (consider both going up and down).
> 7. A company gives its employees a bonus that increases by $100$ dollars each year. If the first-year bonus was $500$, what will be the bonus in the 12th year?
> 8. A staircase is built such that the first step is 10 cm high, the second step is 12 cm high, the third step 14 cm, and so on. Find the total height of the first 20 steps.
> 9. A gardener plants a row of trees such that each tree is 1.5 meters further from the previous one than the previous distance. If the first tree is 2 meters from the start, how far from the start is the 10th tree?
> 10. In a swimming pool, the depth increases by 0.5 meters for each subsequent meter along its length. If the shallow end is 1 meter deep, find the depth at 12 meters from the shallow end.
