# Powers

## Laws of Exponents
### Product of Powers
- Rule: $a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}$  
- Example: $2^3 \cdot 2^4 = 2^{3+4} = 2^7 = 128$

### Quotient of Powers
- Rule: $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$  
- Example: $\frac{5^6}{5^2} = 5^{6-2} = 5^4 = 625$

### Power of a Power
- Rule: $(a^m)^n = a^{m \cdot n}$  
- Example: $(3^2)^3 = 3^{2\cdot 3} = 3^6 = 729$

### Power of a Product
- Rule: $(a \cdot b)^n = a^n \cdot b^n$  
- Example: $(2\cdot 3)^2 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2 = 4 \cdot 9 = 36$

### Power of a Quotient
- Rule: $\left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^n = \frac{a^n}{b^n}$  
- Example: $\left(\frac{4}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{4^3}{2^3} = \frac{64}{8} = 8$

### Negative Powers
- Numerator: $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$  
  Example: $2^{-3} = \frac{1}{2^3} = \frac{1}{8}$
- Denominator: $\frac{1}{a^{-n}} = a^n$  
  Example: $\frac{1}{2^{-3}} = 2^3 = 8$
- Numerator & Denominator: $\frac{a^{-m}}{b^{-n}} = \frac{b^n}{a^m}$  
  Example: $\frac{2^{-2}}{3^{-3}} = \frac{3^3}{2^2} = \frac{27}{4}$

## Laws of Radicals
### Product Rule
- Rule: $\sqrt[n]{a} \cdot \sqrt[n]{b} = \sqrt[n]{a \cdot b}$  
- Example: $\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{8} = \sqrt{16} = 4$

### Quotient Rule
- Rule: $\sqrt[n]{\frac{a}{b}} = \frac{\sqrt[n]{a}}{\sqrt[n]{b}}$  
- Example: $\sqrt{\frac{9}{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{9}}{\sqrt{4}} = \frac{3}{2}$

### Power Rule
- Rule: $\sqrt[n]{a^m} = a^{m/n}$  
- Example: $\sqrt[3]{8^2} = 8^{2/3} = 4$

### Nested Radicals
- Rule: $\sqrt[n]{\sqrt[m]{a}} = \sqrt[nm]{a}$  
- Example: $\sqrt{\sqrt[3]{16}} = \sqrt[6]{16}$

### Radical as a Exponent
- Rule : $\sqrt[n]{a}=a ^{\frac{1}{n}}$
- Example: $\sqrt[3]{27} = 27^{1/3}$

### Root of a Power
- Rule: $\sqrt[n]{a^{m}} = a ^{\frac{m}{n}}$
- Example: $\sqrt[4]{\sqrt[3]{16}} = \sqrt[12]{16}$

### Simplifying Radicals
- Example: $\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \cdot 2} = \sqrt{25} \cdot \sqrt{2} = 5\sqrt{2}$
