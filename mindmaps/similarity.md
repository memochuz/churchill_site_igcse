# 📝 Similarity

## Definition
* **Concept**: Two shapes have the same form but different sizes.
* **Properties**:
    * Corresponding angles are **equal**.
    * Corresponding sides are in the **same ratio**.
* **Visual**: One shape is an enlargement or reduction of the other.

## Scale Factor (SF)
* **Definition**: The constant ratio between corresponding sides.
* **Formula**: $\text{Scale Factor} = \frac{\text{larger side}}{\text{smaller side}}$.
* **Application**: Every side of the smaller shape is multiplied by the SF to get the larger shape.

## Relationship with Areas and Volumes
### Areas
* **Property**: The ratio of areas is the **square** of the scale factor.
* **Formula**: $(\text{Scale Factor})^{2} = \frac{\text{Larger Area}}{\text{Smaller Area}}$.
### Volumes
* **Property**: The ratio of volumes is the **cube** of the scale factor.
* **Formula**: $(\text{Scale Factor})^{3} = \frac{\text{Larger Volume}}{\text{Smaller Volume}}$.
